### Attribution status

The supplied SVG files do not contain author, source URL, or license metadata. Before distributing the app, record the following for the pack:

- Creator name
- Original Flaticon asset or pack URL
- Download date
- Free or Premium license type
- Exact attribution text, when required
- Saved license certificate or purchase receipt

Do not mark these icons as cleared for release until those details are recorded. The application may recolor the icons at runtime, but editing an asset does not remove its original license requirements.
